You are responsible for what happens to you. 

Your mindset is the set of beliefs and attitudes you have internalized about yourself, they shape how you think, act, and respond to challenges. 

**Fixed Mindset**

- Avoids challenges
- Gives up easily
- Doesn't trust his/her instincts

**Growth Mindset**

- Embraces challenges
- Persists through difficulty
- Trusts his/her instincts

Your **Vision** is how you imagine yourself at some point in the future. A personal **Strategy** is your plan for accomplishing your vision. Tactics are day-to-day actions needed to implement your strategy. 

Example:

- Vision: Become a famous professor. 
- Mid-Term strategy: Get tenure in 3 years. 
- Tactic: Write 5 journal papers this year.
